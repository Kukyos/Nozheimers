Records used

All organisms ranked

Orthologs: NCBI Gene ortholog calls (Datasets API) mapped to UniProt; invertebrates use reviewed UniProt homologs because NCBI makes no ortholog call. Alignment: global, BLOSUM62, gap −11/−1. Superposition: Kabsch on Cα of residues with pLDDT ≥ 70 in both AlphaFold models; multi-domain proteins are fitted on their best-fitting domain. Drug pockets: heavy atoms within 4.5 Å in RCSB PDB entries 6LQG, 6LR4, 7C9I, 7D8X, 7Y5T, 6CO3, 4XXD, 5VZY, 5CSZ. Structures are AlphaFold predictions unless marked experimental.
