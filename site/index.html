APP, PSEN1 and PSEN2 across five mammals

Research question: how conserved are the three early-onset Alzheimer's genes across human, mouse, rat, dog and cattle, and do curated disease mutations fall on conserved residues and structured regions of the proteins?

Databases and tools

  • NCBI Gene and RefSeq: reference sequences and orthologs
  • NCBI BLASTp: ortholog check against look-alike genes
  • Clustal Omega (EMBL-EBI): multiple sequence alignment
  • ClinVar: curated human variants
  • UniProt: domains and membrane topology
  • RCSB PDB: experimental structures
  • AlphaFold DB: predicted full-length structures

Rules fixed before any data was pulled

  • Human reference = the MANE Select transcript and its protein.
  • Per species, the RefSeq isoform that aligns best to human (BLOSUM62); a curated NP_ record wins if within 5%.
  • Protein sequences only; each gene analysed separately.
  • % identity = identical residues / columns where both sequences have a residue.
  • ClinVar: missense, Pathogenic or Likely pathogenic, reviewed (criteria provided or better), Alzheimer condition listed.
  • A variant is mapped only if its transcript and reference residue match the human reference.

Interpretation

What the outputs say, with every number computed from the tables above.

Limits of this analysis

Conservation is one line of evidence

A mutation at a conserved residue is consistent with harm, but pathogenicity is established clinically. ClinVar classifications can change.

Not diagnostic

This compares database sequences. It predicts nothing about any individual.

Five species

A small, predefined set. Distant species would separate truly invariant residues from merely mammal-conserved ones.

Structures cover part of each protein

Cryo-EM maps resolve the folded core only. AlphaFold fills the gaps, but its flexible regions are low-confidence predictions.
